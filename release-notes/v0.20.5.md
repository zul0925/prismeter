## 中文

### 用量趋势与耗尽预估

- 指标趋势卡片现在会根据 Prismeter 保存的远端快照，估算百分比额度达到上限或货币余额用尽的时间。
- 仅在同一指标已有至少 3 个、跨度超过 30 分钟的快照后才会给出估算；数据不足时会明确说明还需积累的快照数量。
- 对含有官方恢复时间的额度，结果会区分“会在恢复前耗尽”和“恢复前预计不会耗尽”。
- 累计用量等不代表可用余额的指标只展示趋势，不会被误判为额度预测。

### 可信提示

- 所有预测均展示平均消耗速度、快照样本数或恢复时间，并明确标注为趋势估算，而不是平台承诺。

## English

### Usage trends and exhaustion estimates

- Trend cards now estimate when a percentage quota may reach its limit or a monetary balance may run out, based on Prismeter's saved remote snapshots.
- An estimate is shown only after at least three snapshots of the same metric spanning more than 30 minutes; otherwise the card clearly explains how much more data is needed.
- When an official quota reset time is available, Prismeter distinguishes between exhaustion before reset and an estimate that remains safe until reset.
- Cumulative-usage metrics that do not represent available balance continue to show trends only and are never misclassified as quota forecasts.

### Trustworthy guidance

- Every estimate shows its average consumption rate, sample basis, or reset time, and is labeled as a trend estimate rather than a platform guarantee.
