## 中文

### 新功能

- 新增通用远端指标历史，覆盖 OpenAI/Codex、火山方舟和 DeepSeek。
- 平台页新增“指标趋势”，可在产品主指标、额度、Token、请求量和余额等实际返回的数值之间切换。
- 支持查看最近 24 小时、7 天、30 天和 90 天的变化曲线。

### 数据原则

- 仅采集平台远端实际返回且能可靠识别的数值，不读取本地会话，也不把文本状态、日期或复合字段伪装成指标。
- 常规指标最多每小时采样一次；检测到百分比额度明显重置时会立即记录。
- Xiaomi MiMo 不纳入新的历史与趋势功能；现有旧连接仅保留兼容读取。

### 数据管理

- 指标快照遵循设置中的本地历史保留期限，并随账户删除或“清除历史记录”一起清理。
- 设置页会分别显示指标快照、余额兼容快照和同步记录数量。
- 新增数值解析、采样、额度重置、范围筛选和历史清除回归测试。

## English

### New

- Added normalized remote metric history for OpenAI/Codex, Volcengine Ark, and DeepSeek.
- Platform pages now include metric trends for actual numeric values such as primary usage, quotas, tokens, request counts, and balances.
- Trends can be viewed over the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days.

### Data policy

- Prismeter only samples numeric values reliably identified in remote responses. It does not read local conversations or reinterpret text statuses, dates, or ambiguous compound fields as metrics.
- Regular metrics are sampled at most once per hour; a significant percentage reset is recorded immediately.
- Xiaomi MiMo is excluded from new history and trend work. Existing legacy connections remain readable for compatibility.

### Data management

- Metric snapshots follow the configured local history retention period and are removed with their account or by the clear-history action.
- Settings now report metric snapshots, legacy balance snapshots, and synchronization records separately.
- Added regression coverage for parsing, sampling, quota resets, range filtering, and history deletion.
