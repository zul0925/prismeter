## 中文

### English 覆盖扩展

- 英文界面现在会自动覆盖每次动态生成的账户列表、同步状态、提醒、趋势卡片、连接表单和操作按钮。
- 切换语言后再次同步、切换页面或打开弹窗时，新内容也会使用当前语言，不再只翻译初始页面。
- 完善常用状态、余额字段、更新和账户管理操作的英文词条。

## English

### Expanded English coverage

- English now also applies to dynamically rendered account lists, sync state, alerts, trend cards, connection forms, and action buttons.
- Newly rendered content continues to use the selected language after syncing, changing views, or opening dialogs.
- Added translations for common status, balance, update, and account-management actions.
