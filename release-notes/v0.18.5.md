## 中文

- 设置页新增“本地历史”，可选择保留 30、90、180 或 365 天。
- 修改保留时间后会立即裁剪过期的余额快照与同步运行记录。
- 新增应用内历史清理确认，可单独删除历史而不影响账户、凭据、设置和当前远端数据。
- 设置页显示当前保存的余额快照与同步记录数量。
- 增加历史裁剪及数据隔离回归测试。

## English

- Added Local History settings with 30, 90, 180, and 365-day retention options.
- Changing retention immediately prunes expired balance snapshots and sync events.
- Added an in-app confirmation flow to clear history without affecting accounts, credentials, settings, or current remote data.
- Settings now show the number of stored balance snapshots and sync events.
- Added regression coverage for retention and deletion isolation.
