## 中文

### 额度恢复时间

- OpenAI/Codex 的官方主周期重置时间现在作为结构化字段保存，而不是只保留格式化文本。
- 当 Codex 出现额度提醒时，行动中心和 Windows 托盘会显示预计恢复倒计时。
- OpenAI 平台卡片会优先显示额度恢复时间；平台未提供结构化时间时不会猜测。

### 更新检查体验

- 修复“检查更新”按钮的加载图标：加载圈现在固定尺寸并与文字居中对齐，不再出现错位或异常拉伸。

## English

### Quota recovery time

- OpenAI/Codex primary-window reset times are now retained as structured data instead of only formatted text.
- When a Codex quota alert is active, the action center and Windows tray show a recovery countdown.
- OpenAI platform cards prioritize the quota recovery time; Prismeter does not guess when a platform does not provide a structured timestamp.

### Update-check experience

- Fixed the loading indicator on the Check for Updates button: the spinner now has a stable size and aligns with its label without stretching or shifting.
