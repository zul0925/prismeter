## 中文

### 行动中心

- 总览新增行动中心，只显示最多 3 项未暂缓且需要立即处理的同步、时效、余额或额度事项。
- 同步和数据时效问题可直接从行动中心发起同步；余额和额度问题可跳转查看提醒或调整规则。
- 没有待处理事项时会明确显示“当前无需处理”。

### 暂缓提醒修复

- 暂缓余额、额度等提醒后，它们仍保留在提醒中心供查看和恢复，但不再计入首页、托盘或行动中心的待处理状态。
- 暂缓到期或手动恢复提醒后，相关事项会重新参与健康判断与提醒显示。

## English

### Action center

- The overview now includes an action center that shows up to three unsnoozed items requiring immediate attention: sync, freshness, balance, or quota issues.
- Sync and stale-data issues can be refreshed directly from the action center; balance and quota issues link to their alert or rule settings.
- When there is nothing actionable, the overview explicitly shows that no action is required.

### Snoozed alert fix

- Snoozed balance, quota, and other alerts remain visible in the alert center for review and resuming, but no longer count as pending work on the overview, tray, or action center.
- Once a snooze expires or is manually resumed, the item again participates in health evaluation and alert presentation.
