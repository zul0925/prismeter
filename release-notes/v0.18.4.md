## 中文

- 自动同步失败后会按 2、5、15 分钟进行有限的分级重试，恢复后自动清零失败状态。
- 三次快速重试后恢复到用户设置的常规同步周期，避免对平台产生持续高频请求。
- 同步中心会显示预计重试时间和连续失败次数。
- 账户诊断信息新增下一次重试时间；暂停账户或关闭自动同步时不会执行自动重试。

## English

- Failed automatic syncs now use a bounded 2, 5, and 15-minute retry backoff and reset after recovery.
- After three quick retries, Prismeter returns to the configured regular sync interval to avoid sustained high-frequency requests.
- Sync Center now shows the estimated retry time and consecutive failure count.
- Account diagnostics include the next retry time; paused accounts and disabled automatic sync never trigger automatic retries.
